IMPORTANT: Logo Setup Instructions
===================================

Please save your PEN Club logo image as "logo.jpeg" in this public folder.

The logo should be:
- Named exactly: logo.jpeg
- Format: JPEG
- Recommended size: 500x500 pixels or similar square/rectangular format
- The logo you provided in the chat should be saved here

Once you save the logo as "logo.jpeg" in this folder, it will automatically appear throughout the website:
- In the navbar
- On the home page hero section
- On the about page
- As the favicon in the browser tab

✅ All references have been updated to use logo.jpeg format.
