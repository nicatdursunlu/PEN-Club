IMPORTANT: Logo Setup Instructions
===================================

Please save your PEN Club logo image as "logo.png" in this public folder.

The logo should be:
- Named exactly: logo.png
- Format: PNG (recommended for transparency)
- Recommended size: 500x500 pixels or similar square/rectangular format
- The logo you provided in the chat should be saved here

Once you save the logo as "logo.png" in this folder, it will automatically appear throughout the website:
- In the navbar
- On the home page hero section
- On the about page
- As the favicon in the browser tab

If you need to use a different image format (jpg, svg, etc.), you can update the references in:
- index.html (line 5 for favicon)
- src/components/Navbar.jsx (line 38)
- src/components/Hero.jsx (line 24)
- src/pages/About.jsx (line 32)
